# Employee Document Vault & Asset Tracker — Specification

## Document Vault (`EmployeeDocument.js` & `employeeDocumentEngine.js`)
Stores encrypted employee compliance documents (Aadhaar, PAN, Passport, Driving License, Resume, Contracts, NDAs) with expiry alert tracking.

## Hardware Asset Tracker (`EmployeeAsset.js` & `employeeAssetManager.js`)
Tracks assigned hardware assets (Laptops, Mobiles, SIMs, ID Cards, Vehicles) and handles return/loss status.
